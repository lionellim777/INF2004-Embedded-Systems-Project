AAI10 / STATIONARY BARCODE / V5.7

Read the barcode. Keep the car still.

Both motors are disabled in this firmware. Slide the paper beneath the leftmost barcode sensor; no driving test is available.

Barcode setting: above 250 = black; 220 or below = white. Between these values, keep the previous colour. Keep the paper flat at the normal mounted sensor height.

Connecting to the car…

1 · Start a stationary scan

Leave the car on a stable surface. Keep the printed sheet flat at the normal sensor height, with plain white beneath the sensing tip. Select the test, then press GP20. Wait three seconds for [SCAN READY].

Waiting for live logs.

2 · Slide the whole sheet once

Start with large A. Move the paper smoothly in one direction so the tip crosses every bar and gap, finishing on white. Aim for roughly 5–15 seconds for the whole pattern. No need to pause on each bar. Keep the sensor away from bar ends and the printed letter.

On success, scanning ends with decoded=A valid=1. Z should say Z. If it does not finish, press GP20 again or Finish scan after the sheet has passed completely. A scan also ends after 45 seconds or a data fault.

The log keeps every detected transition, up to 128. Pauses longer than six seconds within a bar or gap reset that decoding attempt, but its edges remain in the result. Uneven hand speed can still prevent decoding; send the log even if it fails.

Do not use the moving Barcode pass from an old browser tab. Refresh to V5.7. This firmware ignores driving commands and keeps motor outputs off. It tests optical capture, not steering or the recurring encoder issue.

Click Finish scan before downloading if the scan is still active. Download after each sheet, before the next scan or power-off. No form to fill in.
